Robin Thomas Design Gallery

Art for Everyday Living

Bespoke furnishings and artful accessories for the home.

Plan a VisitFollow on Instagram

Robin’s travels in search of new and creative collections led to the opening of the gallery, first in Chicago’s River North and, since 2022, in Winnetka’s Hubbard Woods Design District. It is a retail gallery in the truest sense: a place to discover one-of-a-kind pieces, to touch the textiles, and to take home something with a story.

Designed by Robin

Bespoke Furnishings

One-of-a-kind furniture and lighting designed by Robin and made in small numbers by artisan workrooms. Each piece begins the way her interiors do: with a material, a pattern, or a form worth building a room around.

Paris

Maison Lévy

Inspired by a vision to bring art to daily life, Maison Lévy of Paris translates the original works of European painters into creatively patterned textiles, wall coverings and decorative objects for interiors. The extensive collection, with over 100 patterns, brings a unique and artistic sensibility to any interior space.

Maison Lévy pillows, throws, and decorative pieces are available at the gallery.
